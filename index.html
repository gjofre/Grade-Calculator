How Unit Assessments work: the lowest of the three is dropped. The two that remain are each worth 20%. If the final-exam score is higher than a kept UA, it replaces that UA's score (up to both). The final still also counts as its own 20% either way — replacement reuses the final's value, it is not subtracted from the final. Ties break by position (UA 1 first). Blank fields count as 0. Grades are not rounded: 89.99% is a B.
Survey bonus: a flat +0.5 points is added to the final weighted score for the whole class (toggle it off above if needed). It applies before the letter grade, so it can push a borderline score up a bracket, and the displayed score is capped at 100.
